Turn Missed Calls into Booked Jobs

Automated missed-call text follow-up for home service businesses.

What We Do

Lead Recovery Systems sets up a simple automation for your business: when a customer calls your number and you miss the call, they receive an automatic text message so you can keep the conversation alive and win more jobs.

Each client gets their own Twilio and Zapier accounts, connected to a dedicated phone number. Your existing business number simply forwards missed calls into the system—nothing changes for your customers when they dial you.

Who this is for

Home service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, and more).

How it works

  1. A customer calls your business number
  2. If you miss the call, they instantly get a text with clear options
  3. Replies are routed to you/your team so you can book the job

What your customers experience

Customers still call your normal business number—nothing changes on their end.

What’s included

  • Missed-call text flow (during business hours)
  • After-hours flow
  • Routing rules to the right person
  • Basic reporting + support for tweaks
