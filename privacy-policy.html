We may collect information you provide (name, email, phone number, and message details) when you submit a form, email us, or contact our demo/support line. We use information to respond to your inquiry, provide demo/support, troubleshoot, prevent fraud/abuse, and comply with telecommunications requirements.

SMS communications

We do not send promotional SMS marketing. SMS messages are sent only in response to inbound requests (calling/texting our number or submitting a form). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Carriers may require proof of consent. By contacting our demo/support line, you consent to receive SMS related to your inquiry and demo/support. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate (for example, website hosting, email, and messaging providers) only as needed to provide the service.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to respond to requests, provide support, and meet legal/compliance obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.
